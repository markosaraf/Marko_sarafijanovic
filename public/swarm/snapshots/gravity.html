How to play

Defend your planet from incoming asteroids by typing the correct answers before they land. Find a shared set, import from Quizlet, or create your own and invite friends to its leaderboard.

Frequently asked questions

Find out about sets, importing, sharing, accounts, leaderboards and privacy.

What is Quizlet Gravity?

A free flashcard game: type answers to stop asteroids from hitting your planet. Find study sets, share your own and compete with friends. This independent project is not affiliated with Quizlet.

How do I find a study set?

Open Sets and search by title or original Quizlet set creator. Select a set to play or view its leaderboard.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose a guest username to play, import and share sets. Create an account to keep your scores across devices.

How do I import a Quizlet set?

Paste a public Quizlet set link into Import a Quizlet set, then select Import set.

How do I create my own set and how do I edit an existing Quizlet set?

Open Create set to enter terms and definitions or upload a CSV. You need at least two pairs. To edit an existing set, choose Edit a Quizlet set, import its link and change the terms or definitions.

How do I earn points, and what happens when I miss an asteroid?

Answer correctly to earn points. Keep a streak going to earn more. Wrong answers cost 10 points. If you miss an asteroid, type the displayed answer to continue. Miss the same term again and the game ends.

How do I share a set with friends?

Open a shared set and select Copy set link. For your own set, add a title and select Create shared link first.

What is the difference between a guest and an account?

A guest can lose access to their scores if app or browser data is cleared. An account lets you sign back in on any device.

How do I confirm my email or reset my password?

Open the confirmation email after signing up. Check spam or select Resend confirmation if needed. To reset your password, select Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

When does my score appear on the leaderboard?

Choose Play this set on a shared set. When a red asteroid hits your planet and the game ends, your score is saved. Only your highest score for that set appears on the leaderboard.

What information is public?

Shared sets, creator usernames and leaderboard scores are public. Your email and password are never shown publicly. Your own unshared sets stay on your device.

Can I play on my phone or tablet?

Yes, in your browser or the Android and iOS apps. Sign in with the same account to keep your scores across devices.

When are accounts or shared sets removed?

Accounts or sets may be removed for spam or abusive content. Removed accounts appear as Deleted user on leaderboards. Removing a set also removes its leaderboard.
